Hold

Hold a finger anywhere on the screen. Words appear one at a time, right where your eyes already are. Lift your finger, and everything stops at once.

The coloured letter is where to look. Longer words stay a little longer, and every sentence ends with a short breath.

Move

Put two fingers down and slide them sideways. The page opens, and the word follows your fingers, back or forward. Let go, and you read on from there.

Speed

Turn the knob, or slide two fingers up and down. Straight up is six hundred words a minute. Hold the knob for a moment to go back there.

That is all. Add a book, and enjoy it.
