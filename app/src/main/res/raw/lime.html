A lime

At the bottom of a fruit bowl, under three lemons and a very proud orange, lived a small lime.

The lemons talked about the sun. The orange talked about itself. The lime said nothing, because the lime was reading.

It read the label on the jam jar. It read the newspaper wrapped around the potatoes. It read, upside down, the shopping list on the fridge, which said: limes.

Faster

One night the lime found a way to read one word at a time, each word landing exactly where it was already looking. It read a cookbook before midnight, a dictionary before two, and a very long Russian novel before breakfast.

In the morning a hand reached into the bowl, passed the lemons, passed the orange, and picked up the lime.

The lime was not worried. It had read the shopping list. It knew exactly what came next.

The end

You found the lime. Not many do.
