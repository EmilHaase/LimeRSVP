LimeRSVP shows your books one word at a time, right where your eyes already are, with the letter to look at lit up. Hold your finger on the screen to read; lift it and everything stops at once.

<b>Reading that feels natural</b>
<ul>
<li>Reading starts gently and eases up to your speed.</li>
<li>Long, rare and unfamiliar words stay longer; short words pass quickly.</li>
<li>Every comma, sentence and paragraph gets its own pause.</li>
<li>A quick tap goes back to the start of the sentence.</li>
<li>Two fingers sideways open the page and move through the text like a cursor.</li>
<li>Turn the speed knob, or slide two fingers up and down.</li>
</ul>

<b>Your books, properly</b>
<ul>
<li>EPUB, PDF, TXT and HTML, with their real chapters.</li>
<li>Covers, copyright pages and tables of contents are skipped.</li>
<li>Footnotes, page numbers and running headers are left out.</li>
<li>Works with Czech, Polish, German, French and every other Latin-script language, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Japanese and Thai.</li>
</ul>

<b>Easy on the eyes</b>
<ul>
<li>Pure black for OLED screens, night red that keeps your night vision, amber, dim and day themes.</li>
<li>Atkinson Hyperlegible Next as the default font: letters that are hard to mistake.</li>
</ul>

<b>Always know where you are</b>
<ul>
<li>Chapter, progress and an honest estimate of the time left at your speed.</li>
<li>Statistics: real reading speed, time per day, streaks and an optional daily goal.</li>
</ul>

No ads, no tracking and no internet permission: your books never leave your phone.
